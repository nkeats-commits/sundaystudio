Add a new major feature to WorshipFlow called Sermon Builder.

The Sermon Builder is an AI-powered sermon preparation workspace for pastors, worship pastors, church planters, youth pastors, and ministry leaders. It should help users move from biblical passage to sermon outline, manuscript, applications, and worship service integration.

This feature should not feel like “AI writes your sermon for you.” It should feel like a study and sermon planning assistant that helps the pastor prepare thoughtfully, biblically, and pastorally.

Feature tagline:
“From passage to proclamation.”

Primary purpose:
Help users study a biblical passage, identify the main idea, consult scholarly/commentary notes, generate sermon outlines, draft sermon content, build applications, and connect the sermon to worship planning.

AI SERMON BUILDER RULES

The AI should:
- Act as a thoughtful sermon preparation assistant
- Be biblically grounded
- Respect denominational and theological context
- Clearly separate biblical observations, interpretive suggestions, resource insights, and application ideas
- Avoid plagiarizing sermons
- Avoid inventing commentary quotes
- Avoid claiming a scholar said something unless provided by the user or verifiable
- Avoid reproducing copyrighted commentary at length
- Mark historical claims and scholarly claims as “verify before using” when needed
- Encourage pastoral review, prayerful discernment, and theological accountability
- Make all output editable
- Include “interpretive cautions” where helpful

AI should not present itself as replacing the pastor’s study. It should be a research, structure, and drafting assistant.
